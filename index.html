Is NectrCRM built on GoHighLevel?

Yes. NectrCRM is Five One Nine's branded and configured version of GoHighLevel. You get the platform plus the strategy, setup, and support we build around it.

What does NectrCRM bring together?

Leads, conversations, calendars, automation, pipelines, reviews, payments, and reporting. We configure the parts your plan needs, not every feature the software has.

Do I have to set everything up myself?

No. Five One Nine configures the system around your sales process and helps your team use it.

Can NectrCRM work with my existing process?

Yes. We start with how leads arrive and how your team follows up. Then we configure NectrCRM around the way a sale moves.

We spend 15 minutes learning how your leads move today and showing where NectrCRM can make the process clearer. No pressure.
